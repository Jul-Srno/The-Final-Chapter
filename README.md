# The Final Chapter
*Some Doors Should Never Be Opened.*

**The Final Chapter** is a short narrative horror game developed in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript as a self-taught learning project.

Descend into an ancient tomb, search for the missing children, and try to survive what lurks behind its doors. Every choice matters, and not every encounter will go as planned.

Designed as a compact horror experience lasting approximately **10–15 minutes**, the game combines a dark atmosphere, sound design, narrative events, and choices with consequences.

## Synopsis

Near a forgotten village stands an ancient tomb belonging to a lost civilization. According to local legends, no one who enters its depths ever returns alive.

The spirit of a young woman is said to haunt the tomb, relentlessly hunting anyone who dares disturb its silence.

Recently, children have begun disappearing from the village. Witnesses claim they were taken toward the ancient burial grounds, but no one knows what truly lies beneath them.

With the children missing and the tomb shrouded in dreadful legends, the old, dried-up well offers a way into a place no one dares to explore.

Before you descend, the village elder—a woman said to be well over a hundred years old—entrusts you with a strange crucifix believed to ward off evil.

But will it be enough?

**Will you make it out alive—or will the tomb write the final chapter of your story?**

## How to Play

Your goal is to find the missing children and escape the tomb alive.

At each level, you will face three doors:

- **The Children:** The path leading toward the missing children.
- **The Stairs:** The way to the next level of the tomb.
- **The Ghost:** A dangerous encounter with the spirit haunting the tomb.

Choose your path carefully. Every decision can affect your progress and your chances of survival.

### Survival Mechanics

- **The Crucifix:** This mysterious object protects you from your first fatal encounter with the ghost. After that, you are on your own.
- **Injuries:** Some encounters can leave you wounded, making your situation more dangerous.
- **Time Pressure:** Certain events trigger a 30-second countdown. You must reach safety or rescue the children before time runs out.
- **Consequences:** Your choices and encounters influence how your journey unfolds and which ending you reach.

The game is designed around uncertainty, limited protection, and the pressure of making decisions without knowing exactly what lies behind each door.

## Gameplay and Features

- **Atmospheric Horror:** A dark, gothic setting built around an ancient tomb, supernatural folklore, and unsettling encounters.
- **Short Horror Experience:** A focused adventure designed to last approximately 10–15 minutes.
- **Door-Based Exploration:** Choose between different paths as you descend deeper into the tomb.
- **Limited Protection:** The crucifix is your only safeguard against a fatal encounter with the ghost.
- **Injury and Countdown Events:** Certain encounters introduce additional danger and time-sensitive decisions.
- **Multiple Endings:** Your decisions and progression can lead to different outcomes.
- **Hidden Details and Events:** Exploration and replaying the game may reveal elements you missed on your first attempt.
- **Replayability:** Different choices and encounters offer reasons to experience the game more than once.
- **Accessible Interaction:** Keyboard navigation and screen-reader-oriented narration were considered throughout development.

Some events and interactions may vary depending on the player's choices and accessibility settings.

## Accessibility

Accessibility was an important consideration throughout development.

The game incorporates accessibility-oriented practices, including semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader-oriented narration. Particular attention was given to communicating important events and making the experience usable through different forms of interaction.

The game was tested with **TalkBack, NVDA, Windows Narrator, and Orca** to evaluate its behavior across different screen readers. Development also followed **WCAG guidelines where reasonably possible**, with accessibility considered as part of the implementation process rather than treated as an afterthought.

Although these efforts helped identify and address accessibility concerns, behavior may still vary depending on the assistive technology, browser, and individual configuration. The game has not undergone a formal accessibility audit.

## Development

The Final Chapter was created as both a learning project and an opportunity to build a complete interactive experience from concept to release.

Developing the game provided practical experience with:

- JavaScript state management and event handling.
- Branching narrative logic and player choices.
- Victory and defeat conditions.
- Multiple endings and progression systems.
- Responsive layouts and interface design.
- Transitions, sound integration, and atmospheric presentation.
- Code organization and maintainability.
- Accessibility considerations and screen-reader testing.

Beyond the gameplay itself, the project helped me explore how relatively simple mechanics can create tension through uncertainty, limited resources, and meaningful consequences.

The codebase was also designed with extensibility in mind, making it possible to explore additional events, objects, gameplay variations, or levels in the future.

## Artistic Direction and Credits

The game's visual direction draws inspiration from gothic horror, supernatural folklore, and the atmosphere of classic macabre stories.

Visual assets were created using generative AI tools as part of the project's artistic process.

This is an independent, non-commercial project developed by a self-taught developer without a dedicated budget.

Credits for third-party assets and tools are included in the game and can be discovered upon reaching the True Ending.

This first chapter is complete, from its initial concept through implementation and release. It is intended to stand as a playable experience in its own right, rather than an unfinished prototype.
